# Bitcoin Halving Cycle Fractals & On-Chain Supply Metrics

## Overview
Every Bitcoin halving event redefines supply issuance dynamics. By overlapping price performance curves relative to the halving block height across 2012, 2016, 2020, and 2024, fractal patterns offer structural timelines for market tops and accumulation bottoms.

---

## Key On-Chain Metrics

### 1. MVRV Z-Score & Market Extremes
* Historical tops occur when MVRV Z-Score exceeds **5.0 to 6.0**.
* Accumulation windows consistently coincide with Z-Score readings below **0.1**.

### 2. Long-Term Holder Supply (LTH)
* Supply held by entities for >155 days acts as the primary absorption mechanism during parabolic phases.

## Executive Summary
Understanding macro liquidity is paramount to predicting secular market trends. By mapping current central bank monetary policy against historical financial crises and expansionary cycles (1970s, 2000, 2008, 2020), this report identifies key inflection points for global asset allocation.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Easing Pivot** | 1995 / 2019 | Central Bank Balance Sheet Expansion | Risk-On Rally & Capital Inflow |

## 2. Asset Performance Across Liquidity Regimes

## 3. Strategic Portfolio Takeaways

* **M2 Expansion Correlation:** Risk assets historically lag global M2 growth by approximately **60 to 90 days**.
* **Structural Inflation Drivers:** Long-term commodity cycles continue to shadow the 1970s structural inflation framework.
* **Capital Allocation Strategy:** Overweight scarce monetary assets and high-efficiency productivity infrastructure upon confirmed easing signals.
