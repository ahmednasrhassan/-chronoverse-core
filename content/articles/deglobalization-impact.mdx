# The Impact of Deglobalization on Supply Chains

![Deglobalization supply chain impact](../public/images/articles/deglobalization-impact/1767774882.webp)

Global trade dynamics are undergoing a structural shift. As central banks and institutions adapt, institutional investors must monitor key macroeconomic indicators.

### Key Takeaways
* Supply chain regionalization is accelerating.
* Inflationary pressures remain linked to energy inputs.
* Strategic reserve assets are seeing renewed interest.
